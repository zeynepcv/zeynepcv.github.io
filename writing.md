## Papers

**What Makes a Hero? Children's Relationships with Tales Across Socio-Economic Contexts in Turkey** (2025)
Seminar paper, *Oral Traditions: Remembering/Transmission*, Freie Universität Berlin.
A theoretical groundwork on how class shapes the ways children aged 6–12 in Turkey make meaning from stories, drawing on Bruner, Piaget, Lareau, Zipes and Bacchilega.
[Read online in *The Journal of Digital Humanities and Data Sustainability*](https://14141-dh-sustainability.github.io/our-open-access-journal/zeynep_paper.html)
