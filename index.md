I am an M.A. student in **Cultural Studies (Interdisciplinary Studies of the Middle East)** at Freie Universität Berlin. I hold a B.A. in **Sociology** from Boğaziçi University in Istanbul, and I spent a term as a research trainee at the University of Barcelona (2024–2025).

## Research interests

- Gender politics and the politics of fear in contemporary Turkey
- Childhood, class and storytelling
- Protest movements and political participation in the Middle East and Europe
- Digital humanities and sustainable online archives

## Current project

*From Signature to Withdrawal: An Online Archive of the Istanbul Convention in Turkey (2011–2021)*: a small, sustainable digital archive of the events, actors, documents and protests surrounding Turkey's ratification of and withdrawal from the Istanbul Convention. Built for the course *Digital Humanities and Data Sustainability* (LV 14141) at FU Berlin.
